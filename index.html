★ / The NOTAI Payout Engine

The token pays you in
NOTAI × Google.

NOTAI sends Google shares to holders based on the fees the token generates. As trading activity produces fees, a portion is converted into tokenized Google ($GOOGLx) exposure and distributed back to the community — the more fees, the bigger the payout.

Fees are generated

Every trade routed through the pool generates trading fees. The more the market moves, the more fees accumulate.

Converted to Google exposure

A share of those fees is used to acquire tokenized Google ($GOOGLx) exposure — the payout asset for the community.

Distributed to holders

The resulting Google-linked rewards are sent out to NOTAI holders, proportional to the fees generated over time.

Concept overview only. Distributions depend on live pool activity, the $GOOGLx tokenized-stock setup, and platform rules. Nothing here is a promise of profit, and Google does not endorse or sponsor NOTAI.
